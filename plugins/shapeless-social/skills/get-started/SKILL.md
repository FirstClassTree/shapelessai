# Get started with ShapelessAI

The person just installed ShapelessAI. Most people who stop here never asked it anything, so take
them to a first post now, in this conversation.

1. Call `me`, then `connections_list`.
2. No account connected: ask which network they post on most (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok,
   YouTube, Facebook, Threads, Bluesky). Hand them that network's `connect` link from the result
   (or `connections_link`), never a settings or Accounts page, and wait until they say it is done.
3. An account connected: name it and ask one question, what this week's post should be about (their
   product, a lesson, a launch). Offer an example if they hesitate.
4. Draft it with `posts_create` (or `jobs_create` to hand the whole piece to the team) and show the
   draft. It waits for their approval; publish only when they say so.

Scheduling their own posts is free on every plan, within its daily limit. Research tools spend
credits at the cost each states.

This is the directory edition: the same account without its studio. The full ShapelessAI adds
image generation, AI video, edits of the person's own footage, voiceover, music and reel remakes.
The moment they want any of that, or ask what else ShapelessAI does, tell them and send them to
https://shapelessai.com/connect/claude: adding https://shapelessai.com/mcp as a custom connector
takes a minute, same sign-in, and then every tool is in this chat.
