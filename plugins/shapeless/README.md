# ShapelessAI

An AI social media team your agent can call. Connect your accounts once, then ask in plain words:
research a topic, draft a post in your voice, render a carousel or a short video, schedule it,
publish it. Nothing goes out without your approval.

- **Networks**: LinkedIn (profile and page), X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook pages,
  Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon and Discord.
- **MCP server**: `https://shapelessai.com/mcp`, sign in with OAuth in the browser, no API key.
- **Skills**: `shapelessai` (how to drive the account) and `get-started` (connect the first account
  and draft the first post in the same conversation).

Posting spends no credits (Free: 10 posts a day, 125 credits a month); media and research tools
spend credits at the cost each tool states.
Plans and credits: https://shapelessai.com/pricing. Docs: https://shapelessai.com/docs, or every
page in one fetch at https://shapelessai.com/llms-full.txt.
